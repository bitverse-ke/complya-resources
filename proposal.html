Based on what you shared about Browns Co's exposure — farmer data across the supply network, milk collection systems, payment flows, and the move into traceability — this proposal scopes a 60-day engagement to get Browns Co from current state to fully registered, audit-ready, and system-risk-clarified under the Data Protection Act, 2019. Fixed fee, three milestone payments, no surprises.
What we propose

Three milestones. Three deliverables. Three payments.

Weeks 1–3 01

ODPC registration

  • Data audit & classification — what personal data you hold, where, lawful basis, retention
  • ODPC controller registration — application, supporting filings, certificate secured
  • Statutory standing — registered in the company name, annual returns scheduled
Weeks 3–6 02

Policy layer + system risk review

  • Privacy policy, cookie policy, data retention schedule — board-grade, ODPC-aligned
  • Farmer onboarding consent forms + collection notices — used at intake, not buried
  • Breach response procedure, DPIA template, ROPA — ready for an inspector
  • System risk review: milk collection app, payment system, farmer database, traceability platform — access controls, consent capture, audit logging, retention automation. Reviewed, not rebuilt.
Weeks 7–8 03

Sign-off & ODPC submission

  • Staff training — 1 session, ops + IT teams
  • Walk-through with your designated DPO and ops lead
  • Final ODPC submission pack + handover
What you'll have at the end →

Audit-ready posture

  • ODPC certificate current and in the company name
  • Policy pack that holds up under inspection
  • System risk report ranking privacy gaps by severity
  • Trained ops staff who can answer an inspector's first three questions
  • Estimated 60% reduction in ODPC-enforcement exposure versus current state
Anchor !

Why this is cheap insurance

  • Statutory exposure: KES 5M fine or 1% of annual turnover — whichever is higher
  • For Browns Co, fines alone are several multiples of this engagement fee
  • ODPC enforcement in 2024–2025 has accelerated against exactly this profile of controller
  • This fee is the cost of preventing the conversation we'd rather you not have with ODPC
Out of scope (priced separately) —

What's not in this engagement

  • System rebuilds — we review and recommend, your dev team (or ours on a separate quote) implements
  • Ongoing DPO retainer after the 60 days
  • Defence of an actual ODPC investigation if one is opened before this engagement completes
  • Legal counsel — we deliver the compliance artefacts, your lawyers review if needed
Pricing & payment terms — three milestone payments
  1. Mobilization — 40% (KES 300,000). Payable on signing. Covers data audit, ODPC application work, and project kick-off.
  2. Mid-engagement — 40% (KES 300,000). Payable on delivery of Milestone 2 (policy pack + system risk report). Typically week 5–6.
  3. Sign-off — 20% (KES 150,000). Payable on delivery of Milestone 3 (training + final ODPC submission). End of week 8.
  4. Total fixed fee: KES 750,000. Plus applicable taxes. No hourly billing. Scope changes priced by mutual agreement.
  5. Timeline: 60 calendar days from mobilization. Delays caused by Browns Co (system access, document provision, scheduling) extend timeline but not fee.
  6. Validity: This proposal is valid for 30 days from the date on the cover letter.
